# Evidence and Privacy Policy

This repository is designed as a transparent public record of a coursework-scale prototype while protecting restricted, private, and third-party material.

## Publicly documented

- research objective and system architecture;
- certificate-based identity and permissioned-ledger design;
- trust-aware access-control workflow;
- separation between the IoV prototype and later Fabric/Kubernetes work;
- high-level deployment and evaluation activities;
- limitations and public-claim boundaries.

## Retained privately

- the complete coursework report;
- raw screenshots that may contain infrastructure identifiers;
- interactive Cisco Packet Tracer activity files;
- full chaincode and environment configuration;
- credentials, connection details, and cloud-resource identifiers;
- course-provided or third-party assets;
- raw evaluation artifacts not yet prepared for independent reproduction.

## Numerical claims

Quantitative figures from the private coursework report are not promoted as independently revalidated research results in this repository. A number should only be added publicly when its configuration, data, logs, and calculation procedure can be documented sufficiently for review.

## Historical integrity

The original project period and the later public-archive date are kept separate. The repository does not fabricate historical Git activity or imply that public documentation existed during the original coursework period.
